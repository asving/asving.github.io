The Lean 4 proofs were produced from the blueprint by a fleet of Claude agents on the Prove2Me platform against a pinned Mathlib, then unified and golfed into the single codebase shown here; every main theorem was checked for axioms (propositional extensionality, classical choice, quotient soundness only) and statement preservation, with the certificates linked on the formalization page. Numerical checks of every displayed law against an independent implementation of the recursion accompany the text.
