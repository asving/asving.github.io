How this was made

This paper and its formalization were made in October 2026 under the direction of Asvin G, who posed the problem, set the standards, and verified the main statements. GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI) wrote the informal proof of the type laws and of the reciprocity theorem, the Lean blueprint, and the text of the paper; Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic) orchestrated the work, ran the fresh-context adversarial reviews and revision rounds, and built this site. The Lean 4 proofs were produced from the blueprint by a fleet of Claude agents on the Prove2Me platform against a pinned Mathlib, then unified and golfed into the single codebase shown here; every main theorem was checked for axioms (propositional extensionality, classical choice, quotient soundness only) and statement preservation, with the certificates linked on the formalization page. Numerical checks of every displayed law against an independent implementation of the recursion accompany the text.

This site was first generated on 2026-10-17 by a generator that no longer exists. Since 2026-11-11 the scripts in the site/ directory of the Lean repository rebuild the Lean pages, the Lean panels and the tables. They reproduce the 2026-10-17 pages byte for byte from the v1.1.0 sources. The paper text is kept in the paper page itself.
