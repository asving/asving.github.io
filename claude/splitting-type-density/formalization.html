The formalization: what is proved, and how to trust it

The paper's two main theorems are machine-checked in Lean 4 over Mathlib: Theorem 1.1 for every complete discrete valuation ring with finite residue field, and Theorem 1.2 as identities of rational functions. This page says exactly what the Lean statements are, what a reader has to audit to trust them, which certificates exist, and how far the rest of the paper is formalized.

1. The four main theorems

All four depend only on the axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound (#print axioms, re-run by tools/release_check.sh; log below). The codebase contains no sorry, admit, axiom or native_decide.

main_dvr — SurfaceBlowup/Main.lean:31–51

theorem main_dvr (O : Type) [CommRing O] [IsDomain O]
    [IsDiscreteValuationRing O] [IsAdicComplete (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal O) O]
    [Finite (IsLocalRing.ResidueField O)] [TopologicalSpace O] [IsTopologicalRing O]
    [CompactSpace O] [T2Space O] [SecondCountableTopology O] [MeasurableSpace O] [BorelSpace O]
    (htop : IsAdic (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal O))
    (n : ℕ) (σ : FactorType) :
    SurfaceBlowup.monicDensity O n σ =
        densityValue (monicExpr n σ) (Nat.card (IsLocalRing.ResidueField O)) ∧
    SurfaceBlowup.projectiveDensity O n σ =
        densityValue (projectiveExpr n σ) (Nat.card (IsLocalRing.ResidueField O))

main_dvr is Theorem 1.1: for every complete DVR O with finite residue field (any characteristic, any ramification), with its adic topology, Borel σ-algebra and normalized Haar measure, the monic and projective type probabilities are the values at q = |k| of the universal expressions monicExpr n σ, projectiveExpr n σ (defined by the finite recursion of Sections 6–7). The hypotheses hn, hσ, hdegree are not used by the proof; they are kept so that the statement is, constant for constant, the certified one. Tests/NonVacuity.lean instantiates it at 𝔽_q⟦X⟧.

recip_main — SurfaceBlowup/Reciprocity/Main.lean:278–299

theorem recip_main (n : ℕ) (hn : 0 < n) (σ : FactorType) :
    (monicExpr n σ).rational =
      ((lawCoeff (rootLaw n) σ).subst (.inv .var)).rational ∧
    (projectiveExpr n σ).rational =
      ((projectiveExpr n σ).subst (.inv .var)).rational

recip_main is Theorem 1.2, coefficientwise: A_{n,σ}(q) = B_{n,σ}(1/q) and R_{n,σ}(1/q) = R_{n,σ}(q) in ℚ(q), where B_{n,σ} is the coefficient of the root law rootLaw n (the law of S(1;n), which root_cell_law identifies with the Xn-cluster model).

2. The trust boundary

Lean checks proofs, not statements. To trust the theorems above a reader must audit only:

  1. SurfaceBlowup/Basic.lean (~500 lines): factorization types, the rational expressions Expr, laws, recursion states and tangent patterns, the weights, the recursion stateLaw, the universal expressions monicExpr/projectiveExpr, and the densities monicDensity/projectiveDensity. These are, constant for constant, the owner-signed definitions published as Prove2Me nodes N001–N010 (namespace SurfaceBlowupV2), checked at kernel level by Checks/run_checks.sh.
  2. The statements of the four theorems above (in Main.lean and Reciprocity/Main.lean).

Every other file contains only proofs and auxiliary definitions that do not occur in these statements.

Human sign-off. The definitions (N001–N010) and the statement of main_field_form (Theorem 1.1 for unramified K/ℚp, Prove2Me node N660) were signed off by the owner before the formalization campaign; the statement of recip_main (N1319) was signed off on 2026-10-09. The statement of main_dvr uses only the signed definitions and is kernel-identical to the certified general-DVR statement (MONOLITH_REPORT_general_dvr). [OWNER: confirm whether the main_dvr statement text itself has been signed off.]

How the proof differs from the paper. The formal proof follows the paper's architecture (recursion on blow-up states, Gaussian measure identity, Hensel assembly, contact algebra for reciprocity) but works on the concrete surfaces O⟦T,X⟧/(π − u·T^a·X^b) rather than on all regular surfaces, replaces analytic arguments (analytic normalization, inverse-function theorem, change of variables) by finite-digit congruence and cylinder-set arguments, replaces the formal-scheme cohomology of Section 4 by explicit two-chart Čech complexes, and handles inseparability through type predicates with finite integral closures plus a Haar-null bridge (orig_monicDensity_eq). Nothing is cited from the literature: everything bottoms out in Mathlib.

How this was made

This paper and its formalization were made in October 2026 under the direction of Asvin G, who posed the problem, set the standards, and verified the main statements. GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI) wrote the informal proof of the type laws and of the reciprocity theorem, the Lean blueprint, and the text of the paper; Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic) orchestrated the work, ran the fresh-context adversarial reviews and revision rounds, and built this site.
